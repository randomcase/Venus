the honest part

“Wanted” is the biggest tier and that is the finding. 30 projects are real work that nothing currently points at. That is a statement about the dependency graph and not about the work — but it does mean a consolidation would mostly be moving things that nothing was asking for, which is worth knowing before starting rather than halfway through.
Archive is not delete. The four silences — no README, no build, no pages, nothing pointing at it — mean nobody has needed it since it was made. Any one of those alone is normal. All four together is the signal, and the correct response is to move it in and stop maintaining it separately, not to throw it away.
What a move actually costs. Every project brought in loses its own history unless it comes via git subtree add or a filter-repo rewrite. Losing it is sometimes right and is never free, and the 34 projects with uncommitted changes have to be settled first — a move does not carry a dirty tree.
