Ten miles is not an arbitrary span. Down five and you are under the cloud in the pre-cloud haze at almost a hundred degrees. Up five and you are inside the layer that holds the unknown ultraviolet absorber — the thing that takes 320 to 500 nm out of the sunlight, that has been measured since the 1920s, and that nobody has identified. Half the stations on this climb are inside an open question, which is a good ratio for an instrument.

2 · The register of nothings what was looked for, and was not there

This repository holds thirty-one knowledge-base entries and every one of them records something established. Nothing anywhere records what was searched and came back empty — which in real science is most of the work and nearly all of the honest part. Mile one, nothing. Mile two, nothing. That is data. It rules things out, it costs exactly as much to collect, and it is the half nobody publishes because nobody is paid for it.

identified is declared, incremented by nothing, and printed — the same instrument as named on the bench and gaps on the quiet storm. It reads zero because after a hundred years of looking, that is the honest number. Every line above is +1 exactly as a finding would be, because ruling a candidate out at a stated sensitivity costs the same and is worth the same, and a base that only counts hits is a base that will quietly stop reporting misses.

The fair, and the name on the fuselage

St. Louis put on a world’s fair in 1904 and offered a hundred thousand dollars for a flight around a closed course. Nobody collected it. The aeronautic exhibits were mostly balloons and mostly disappointing, and the thing being debuted did not work.

Twenty-three years later a monoplane crossed from New York to Paris, and it was called the Spirit of St. Louis — because a group of St. Louis businessmen had put up the money and the name of the backers went on the fuselage and over the ocean. The fair did not produce the aeroplane. It produced the people who paid for one.

Which is the argument for showing the thing before it works, and for the register above. You do not debut a result. You debut a traverse — ten miles, eleven stations, six nothings and a stated sensitivity — and what you are actually publishing is the method and the empty rows, so that the next person does not spend their years on the same absent sulfur. Join the guild, rise through the ranks, and confer. The conferring is the whole of it: a guild member who publishes freely has broken the guild’s rent, and that is the only betrayal that leaves everybody better off, including the people betrayed.
