A merchant carries cargo and nothing else. An auxiliary carries the fleet — fuel, stores, spares, and the rig to pass them across to a hull under way. A monitor is neither: almost no freeboard, almost no speed, and the heaviest belt and gun the yard can put on a hull that shallow.

The merchant is the peer interface, and it is perpendicular to the line of march: it runs cargo toward the rear guard, never out from it, and it is never closer than three days nor further than six. Because it sits off the line it carries its own authority — a commandant, an old sergeant, kept on past the point where he was still doing the walking. He is AI now. The corporal holds the rear guard; the commandant is what the corporal answers to when the general is out of reach.
