Findings on the record six, with the lines they rest on

17–18
Matriculation is not a vote.

Stated by the appellant as fact, not as argument, and never retracted. The whole of In re the Pot is the question of whether this sentence is law or merely true.

36–40
The pot, and where the money goes.

Funds in; split, dashed and dined. The stated purpose of the gathering is food. Nothing in the record describes a moment at which anyone was asked anything, and the court below inferred the asking from the paying.

41–42
Fifteen years of abstention, entered by the abstainer.

The appellant is the ninth chair and says so on the record. The holding below converts this into fifteen years of consent. The bench declines to, on all twelve circuits, including the ones it loses.

24, 29–33
Cohesive body, factional parts, hats rather than people.

Less amoeba than germ; identitarianly aligned rather than identical; not love, not sex, not survival. This is why the register at seats 2 and 5 of the Pulse is left open instead of netted off — one hand, two hats, and on two circuits it votes against itself and both votes count.

8, 10, 15
Practice, not preference.

A vote of biases and practice; not a rigid electorate; a practised vote. Every seat on both boards answers to an instrument rather than to persuasion, and no control on either page can change anybody's mind. That was a specification, not a limitation of the medium.

13
The motion itself.

just hey how are you vote. Six words, entered between two other things, and the only line in the record that says what the court is ultimately for. Motion 12 is that line with the grammar put back and nothing else added.

Not in evidence

The transcript contains four links the bench did not rely on — martial arts, superhero fiction, fantasy, action fiction — and two on the Toei Company and Hurricaneger, from which the court took its shape and nothing more: three benches of three, cohesive and factional, which is the sentai arrangement and also the syndicate's, and the page is aware that this is the joke. No holding rests on any of them. The appellant's age is on the record and is not material; the fifteen years are.
