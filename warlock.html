The warlock

a siege that does not end

The field is Krak des Chevaliers at its own dimensions — concentric, outer 200×140 m, inner 140×100, the inner wall higher so taking the outer ring puts you in a corridor overlooked from both sides. The numbers on the board are the ones siege.mjs computed: towers 40 m apart against a 100 m bowshot, 8.3 m of dead ground at the foot where nothing can shoot down, and a 25.9 m ladder to climb it.

the field twin towers, two leaves, and 8.3 m nobody can reach

Red is dead ground. Inside 8.3 m of the wall nothing can shoot down — that is the geometry, not a rule somebody added. The Digger stands there on purpose. Only a Ward or a Bolt reaches inside it.

the forge make one of your own

A spell with no stated cost is a button. The generator refuses one at build time and the forge refuses it here, at the same threshold — so anything you forge would also survive node warlock.mjs.
And power must be paid for. The forge scores what the spell actually does and refuses any mana cost below it. You can build something enormous. You cannot build something enormous and cheap.

the one rule

You summon demons to hold a wall against demons, so a summon is the same kind of thing as the thing outside. Fifteen per cent turn. Not on a timer and not when you are ready — on the roll. A turned summon keeps its tower and stops being yours, and you are then holding a defended position against yourself.
Unmake is the only spell that undoes one. It costs 9 of a pool that refills slowly and it takes exactly one thing, so a turn is survivable rather than summoning being safe.

the Herald the only one taken from the record

It does not fight. It walks to the gate carrying a letter, and if it arrives the gate opens, because the garrison believes it. No wall on this board stops it and none of the arithmetic applies.

Krak des Chevaliers fell in 1271 to exactly that — a forged order to surrender, delivered to a garrison that believed it. The best numbers on the siege board were beaten by a document.
