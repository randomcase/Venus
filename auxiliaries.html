Sustainment, days

Sustainment is a sum, not an average — it is the total of the days each sustainment hull carries, which is only the endurance of the train if the fleet needs one store at a time. It does not. Fuel, water, provisions and ordnance run down together, and the train is finished when the first of them is: the shortest line in that column, not the sum of them. The board reports the sum because CSS can add and cannot take a minimum, and the commandant is expected to know the difference.

The hulls that have to come alongside under way keep station with the line. The ones that need the fleet stopped sit astern of it. The tug and the hospital ship stay with the rear guard because that is where ships fall out, and the corporal answers for both. The merchant is off the line entirely and always has been.
