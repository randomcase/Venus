three aspects · one auxiliary

The fourth lamp is the auxiliary channel: one job at a time, each in its own colour. Not a lamp that means four things — a lamp that means exactly one and says which.

The cut two actions, and it is not an aspect

Reversible, and it announces itself. Arming is how the lane finds out somebody is about to do this.
Out of reach until armed — by mouse and by keyboard both. Nothing on this board restarts the lane afterwards.
Safe. The cut is unarmed and the throw cannot be reached. The file runs on the aspect showing.
Armed. Nothing has happened and the lane has not changed. The difference between this and safe is entirely social — somebody has declared an intention, in the open.
Cut. The file is stopped where it stood, the aspect lamps are dark because a speed is not an answer to a stop, and the auxiliary lamp is flashing red over whatever it was doing.

Why 2B is absent and not zero. The arrival end is not mine to consider, so it is not in the total — and the honest way to exclude something is to name it in the table and leave the cell empty, not to quietly multiply by one. A missing factor written as 1 is a claim that the arrival end has exactly one state, which is a much stronger statement than "I did not count it" and happens to be false.

What the accounted figure is worth. 34,992 is the number of distinct configurations this lane can be in, counting only what is set on this page, the Earth-side departure and whether the relay is holding or forwarding. It is a state count, not a difficulty measure — two-thirds of those states differ only in what a stage charges, and nobody has to think about most of them. The three that matter are safe, armed and cut.

What was taken out

Every blocker, on instruction. The red aspect is gone, the four station stops are gone, the master stop is gone, and demurrage went with them — nothing is held, so nothing accrues for being held. The file runs from the moment the page opens.

And then the cut came back. Not as any of those. The station stops were three levels of the same idea and the red aspect was a speed pretending to be a stop; the cut is neither. It is one control, outside the aspect code, behind two actions, and it is the only thing on this board that can end the run.

That is a real change and it is worth being clear about it, because the board was built the other way round: three levels of stop, each one above the last, specifically so that no state could be entered that something above it could not end. That property is now gone. It is one commit back if it is wanted, and it does not need rebuilding.

What is left is the point. One lane. One file of eight, nose to tail, the same run started at eight different moments. One control over all of them, and three settings, none of which is a hold. Four stages taking their tariff as the file passes, and one number at the bottom that is the whole of what the lane earns on a pass. Every figure here is a CSS counter and there is no script in this file.
