what it is

The sky is named in Arabic and most people using the names do not know it. Aldebaran is al-dabarān, the follower, because it follows the Pleiades across the sky. Betelgeuse is yad al-jawzā, the hand of the giant. Rigel is the foot. Vega is al-nasr al-wāqi, the falling eagle, and Altair is al-nasr al-ṭāir, the flying one. Deneb is the tail. Algol is al-ghūl. Fomalhaut is the mouth of the fish. Around two hundred of the star names in use are Arabic.

the system

The 28 lunar mansions — manāzil al-qamar — divide the ecliptic into the moon's nightly stations rather than the sun's twelve. A lunar calendar needs the moon's position, not the sun's, so the partition follows the body that actually marks the month.

on the record

Al-Sufi's Book of Fixed Stars, 964 CE, revised Ptolemy against observation and carries the first known descriptions of the Andromeda galaxy and the Large Magellanic Cloud.

the instrument

The astrolabe is an analogue computer: it solves for time, latitude, star positions and the direction of prayer from one engraved plate and a rotating rete. The qibla problem in particular — the great-circle bearing to a single point on a sphere from anywhere on it — is spherical trigonometry, and al-Bīrūnī's work on it advanced the mathematics itself.

This is the tradition the yard leans on hardest, because the notebook already computes an ecliptic and places Venus on it, and those are Arabic star names on the cover whether or not anybody labelled them.

what it is

Wu xing is usually mistranslated as five elements, which makes it sound like a shorter periodic table. It is five PHASES — wood, fire, earth, metal, water — and the point is not the five things but the two cycles running between them.

the system

GENERATING: wood feeds fire, fire makes earth as ash, earth bears metal, metal carries water as condensate, water nourishes wood. OVERCOMING: wood parts earth, earth dams water, water quenches fire, fire melts metal, metal cuts wood. Every phase both produces one and is checked by another, so nothing in the system can run away — which is a control loop, drawn two thousand years before anybody had the word.

on the record

The directions come with it and include a fifth: east is wood and green, south is fire and red, the CENTRE is earth and yellow, west is metal and white, north is water and black. Four compass points and the place you are standing.

the instrument

The compass is the geologist's instrument and it was Chinese first — the south-pointing spoon, a lodestone on a bronze plate, used for siting buildings and graves for centuries before anybody took one to sea. It was an instrument for reading the ground before it was one for crossing water.

Used here for materials and terrain because that is what it is actually good at: a model of things turning into other things, with a brake on every arrow.

what it is

The youngest tradition on this board by about two thousand years, and the only one that begins in the Americas. It arose in Jamaica following the 1930 coronation of Haile Selassie I as Emperor of Ethiopia, in a colony where an African emperor crowned in an uncolonised African state was not a small fact.

the system

The colours are the Ethiopian tricolour: red, gold and green, with black added for the people. They mean what they mean BECAUSE they are that flag — the flag of the one African state never made a colony — and not because they are a pleasing arrangement. Livity is the life-force and the practice of living rightly with it; ital, from vital, is food kept as close to natural as possible.

on the record

The Lion of Judah is the older symbol underneath: an Ethiopian imperial emblem with a Solomonic lineage claim behind it, carried into a twentieth-century movement rather than invented by one.

the instrument

The Nyabinghi drums — bass, funde, repeater — are liturgy rather than performance. The bass keeps the heartbeat, the funde holds the steady pattern, and only the repeater is free to improvise over them. Two parts hold and one part moves, which is the same shape as every other reliable thing on this board.

These three colours are worn constantly as decoration by people who could not name the flag they come from, and that is worth stating plainly on a board that is about to use them as a palette. They are used here for the new world because the tradition is OF the new world, and the entry names the flag.

Every phase produces one and is checked by another. Wood feeds fire and parts earth; earth bears metal and dams water; water nourishes wood and quenches fire. Both cycles close, and this generator refuses to write the page if either one does not — because a five-phase diagram with a broken arrow is a decoration of a control loop rather than one.

That is the reason to use it for the ground: nothing in the system can run away, and the fifth direction is the place you are standing.
