the husband, the horticulturalist, and the yield

a + b = c + a + bthe relation holds

The husband IS — state, not process; holding title is not an activity. The horticulturalist WILL, and will again — process, not state; the knowledge is exercised and exercising it does not use it up. Together they yield c, and neither is consumed doing it. Seed and labour make a crop and there is still seed and still a labourer.

That is why a and b appear on both sides, and it is the difference between horticulture and extraction. 5 of these 7 persist; 2 are consumed.

The husband

ispersists

He IS. A state and not a process — holding is not an activity, and that is the whole of the role.

Holds the property. Under coverture a married woman had no separate legal standing, so the title, the suit and the forfeiture all run through him.

attacked when: somebody wants what he holds

the recordGiles Corey, Salem Farms, 1692. A prosperous landowner. Under English law refusing to plead meant no trial, no conviction, and therefore no forfeiture of estate. He was pressed under stones for two days and never entered a plea, so his sons inherited.
THAT IS NOT A SUPERNATURAL CASE. It is a probate strategy executed at the cost of a life. He did not die for a belief about the devil; he died to defeat a seizure, and it worked.

The horticulturalist

willpersists

She WILL, and will again. A process and not a state — the knowledge is exercised, and exercising it does not use it up.

Works the ground and the body. Herbs, births, fevers, the timing of sowing. The knowledge is real: nightshade and henbane carry atropine and scopolamine, which are genuinely active and genuinely absorbed through skin, and a root tea that ends a pregnancy ends it.

attacked when: c fails, and the absence needs a name

the recordThe midwives of Saint-Domingue, Martinique and Louisiana. Planters depended on them and feared what they knew. When a plantation infant died of neonatal tetanus or dysentery — or when an enslaved woman ended a pregnancy forced on her — the courts called it poisoning and sorcery.
SHE IS ACCUSED FOR BEING CORRECT. The midwife was in the room when the baby died because she was the person who attended births, and infant mortality was what it was. Presence at the outcome was read as authorship of it, and real pharmacological knowledge was the proof of the pact.

The surplus that a and b make between them, and the only term in the relation that is consumed.

The accuser

takespersists

They TAKE. The one aspect that is neither state nor process but seizure.

Supplies the name that the missing c requires.

attacked when: rarely, and late

the recordAt Salem the accusers were nine to twenty years old: Betty Parris at 9, Abigail Williams at 11, Ann Putnam Jr. at 12 — who named over sixty people and apologised in church in 1706 — and then servants, orphans and frontier refugees at 17 to 20.
THE ACCUSATION RAN FROM THE POWERLESS TO THE POWERLESS. Orphaned servants naming destitute widows. The one moment of consequence available to a person with none, spent on people with less — and the adults who ran the court are the ones who made that lethal.

attacked when: a faction wants the office, or he has become inconvenient

the recordJohannes Junius, burgomaster of Bamberg, burned 1628 — his letter to his daughter survives, written from the cell, saying the confession was false and extracted. Dietrich Flade, a senior judge at Trier, grew sceptical of the trials and was tried and executed himself in 1589.
THE MEN IN THE WARLOCK ARCHIVE ARE NOT POOR. Burgomaster, judge, minister, priest, landowner. When a man was accused it was frequently a factional fight over office or estate; when a woman was accused it was frequently poverty and age. Both are motives. They are not the same motive, and calling both "witchcraft" hides that.

The warlock, as fiction

tradesconsumed

He TRADES. He gives something away and receives power, which is the one aspect nobody in the record has.

Chooses the pact. Commands. Summons. Is feared because he is genuinely dangerous.

attacked when: never seriously — he wins or he is defeated by a hero

the recordNo historical accused person matches this figure. Not one.
THE FICTION INVERTS THE RECORD EXACTLY. The archetype is powerful, male, and chose it. The accused were overwhelmingly powerless, disproportionately women, old or poor, and chose nothing — the only power ever attributed to them was attributed BY the people killing them, and the attribution was the weapon.

The warlock is what gets written afterwards. It hands the dead the power they never had, which reads as flattery and functions as cover: if they were genuinely sorcerers, the trials were a mistake about a real thing rather than a machine for taking property and explaining grief.

The survivor

endurespersists

She ENDURES, which is not the same as winning.

Confesses, because confession was survivable and denial was not.

attacked when: continuously, and then administratively

the recordTituba, enslaved, roughly 30 to 40, owned by Rev. Parris, no civil rights whatsoever. She confessed and survived. She was held in jail fourteen months — and because prisoners were charged for their own imprisonment, she was SOLD to cover her jail fees.
THE LAST DETAIL IS THE ONE TO SIT WITH. She was never convicted. She was held for over a year and then sold to pay the cost of holding her. The supernatural charge collapsed and the accounting did not, which is the clearest statement anywhere of what the machinery was actually for.

two mechanisms, one word

When c fails, they go after b. A dead infant, a failed crop, a sick cow — the absence needs a name, and the name goes to whoever has the knowledge. The midwife was in the room when the baby died because she was the person who attended births. Presence at the outcome was read as authorship of it, and real pharmacological knowledge became the proof of the pact.
When somebody wants a’s property, they go after a. And the men in the warlock archive are not poor: burgomaster, judge, minister, priest, landowner. Junius wrote to his daughter from the cell that the confession was false. Flade was a judge at Trier who grew sceptical and was tried himself.
Both are motives. They are not the same motive. Calling both of them witchcraft is what lets a property seizure and a grief reaction share a vocabulary, and the sharing is not accidental — one word that covers both is more useful to a court than two words that don’t.

the inversion why the fiction points the wrong way

The fictional warlock is the only figure here who trades. He gives something away and receives power. Nobody in the record does that, because nobody in the record had power to receive — the only power ever attributed to them was attributed by the people killing them, and the attribution was the weapon.
So the archetype inverts the evidence exactly. Powerful against powerless, male against disproportionately female, chosen against chosen-for. The fiction hands the dead a power they never had, which reads as flattery and functions as cover: if they really were sorcerers, the trials were a mistake about a real thing rather than a machine for taking property and explaining grief.
