the bench syndicated at two — a lone voice moves nothing

One voice is a monopoly on the reading. The parliament settled that rule and the bench uses it: a single opinion moves nothing, and two concurring move the verdict. That is not a mercy — a lone accuser was dismissible and a pair was not, which is exactly why the afflicted testified together and why the depositions come in matched sets.

the fees signed, accruing, and unpayable

Prisoners were charged for their own imprisonment. Food, the keeper’s wage, the irons. The debt accrued whether or not you were ever convicted, and it did not clear on acquittal.
Every sitting posts a line and each is signed with a real ECDSA P-256 key minted in this browser, non-extractable. The signatures are genuine. The total is not payable, which is the point: the supernatural charge collapses and the accounting does not.

what each answer really did

Deny it — Spectral evidence could not be rebutted. The testimony described a shape only the accuser could see, so there was nothing for the accused to contradict.
Confess, and name others — The one road that survived. Confessors were held rather than hanged; the unrepentant went to the gallows. A confession was expected to produce names, which is exactly how it spread.
Say nothing at all — Giles Corey, Salem Farms, 1692. Refusing to plead meant no conviction, and therefore no seizure of his estate. He was pressed under stones for two days and never entered a plea. His sons inherited. It is a probate strategy executed at the cost of a life.
Ask for the water — Float and the water rejects you: guilty. Sink and you are innocent, and you are also underwater. There is no outcome that clears you and leaves you alive — which is not a flaw in the test, it is the test.

what this refuses

To let you win. There is no verdict on this board that is both survival and innocence, because there was not one. The only road that survived required naming somebody else, and that is not a balance problem to be tuned — it is the finding.
Tituba took that road. She confessed, survived, was held fourteen months, and — because prisoners were charged for their own imprisonment — was sold to cover her jail fees. She was never convicted of anything. The supernatural charge collapsed and the accounting did not.
