2 · The range, argued is turnover worth a building?

The rescope moved the range from fertilising — which it cannot do in a bag — to turnover. That is a weaker-sounding claim and it deserves checking rather than asserting.

path from leaf to available Proughly
Litter → microbial decomposition alone2–5 yr slow, temperature-limited, and it stalls in cool or dry spells
Litter → detritivore gut → excretadays comminution plus gut passage, then microbes finish it fast
Live foliage → herbivore → excreta1–3 d bypasses litterfall entirely — the shortest path there is
Wood → anythingdecades nothing shortens this much, which is why wood is the sink

So the answer is yes, and not narrowly. An animal that eats a leaf and passes it in two days has moved that phosphorus through in about a thousandth of the time decomposition would have taken, and put it back in a labile form. Multiply by a standing herbivore and detritivore community and the litter pool’s residence time falls by roughly an order of magnitude. That is not a decoration on the ecosystem. In a closed system it is the pump, and it is the only pump available, because the nutrient-importing kind needs an outside and there isn’t one.

Which also says what the building is for, and it is not what its first draft said. It is not making fertiliser. It is holding the residence time down, continuously, by keeping a population of things that eat and excrete standing on the ground. Fish in a bag is the entry; templates-build/range.json is the building, renamed the turnover.

3 · The resettlement the forest is not next to the town

Where people live is decided by residence time too, and the logic is short: waste produced far from where it is needed is waste with a long path, and a long path is a long residence time. A single city on one edge of 9,000 square miles concentrates the output and lengthens every return journey. So the settlement is distributed, and it is distributed for an accounting reason rather than an aesthetic one.

the pattern

Nodes, not a capital

Many small settlements inside the canopy rather than one on the rim. Each one’s output enters the cycle where it falls, so the return path is metres instead of hundreds of kilometres. It is the decentralization entry arriving as a town plan.

the cleaning

The forest is the plant

There is no drain. Nothing is taken away because there is no away. Air and water are cleaned by the same system that grows the food, which means the forest is not an amenity beside the settlement — it is the life support, and the settlement is inside it.

the constraint

Distance is a cost in years

Every kilometre between production and uptake is time an atom spends in transit and not in use. The plan that minimises travel is also the plan that minimises residence time, which is unusual: the convenient layout and the correct one are the same layout.

4 · The mutation this is not the thing to be afraid of

Radiation first, because it is the fear and it is misplaced. Venus has no magnetic field at all — which sounds fatal and is not, because at 55 km the column of atmosphere above you is 574 g/cm². Earth at sea level is about 1,033. Earth at 5.5 km — La Paz, Lhasa — is about 500. The deck shields like a high-altitude city, and it does that without a magnetosphere, because half a tonne of CO₂ per square centimetre does not care whether a particle was deflected first. The missing field matters over geological time, for atmospheric loss. It does not matter to a genome standing under 574 g/cm².

The real risk is the bottleneck, and it is arithmetic rather than physics. Heterozygosity is lost at 1/(2Nₑ) per generation, so the founder count decides how much variation still exists a century of generations later.

And the last word is the one that should settle it. You cannot prevent mutation and you should not want to. Nothing you bring evolved for 0.888 g, a four-day light cycle, 574 g/cm² and a closed phosphorus loop — and a forest that cannot change is a forest that fails slowly and correctly. Mutation is not the danger. Mutation without enough variation for selection to sort is the danger, and that is a bottleneck, and a bottleneck is a manifest decision made years earlier by somebody counting founders.

Which puts the fear somewhere useful. The thing to be careful about is not what the radiation does to the twenty-fifth generation. It is how many of the first generation you brought — and for seeds, spores and soil fauna, a thousand founders each across roughly 6,400 species is 6.4 million individuals, which is a seed bank rather than a cargo problem. The expensive rows are the birds and the larger animals, and they are also the shortest rows on the list.
