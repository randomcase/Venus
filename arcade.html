Every board in the yard, in a cabinet, running. Not screenshots — 218 live frames of the real files, counted from the directory so this page cannot go stale. 218 cabinets

The hub drives the arcade, not the cabinets. Without script one document cannot set state in another, so the console moves what is genuinely its own — the attract drift and its own cut — and every board keeps its own controls. That is the right answer anyway: a hub that could silently drive 218 boards is a worse design than 106 boards that each answer for themselves.
