The cabinets are displays, not controls. Each frame has pointer-events:none, so a click lands on the cabinet rather than inside the board — which stops you changing a board's state by accident while looking at seventeen of them. Use open to work one properly.

The hub drives the arcade, not the boards. Without script one document cannot set state in another, so the console moves the wall — attract, focus, and its own cut — and every cabinet keeps its own controls. That is the right answer anyway.
