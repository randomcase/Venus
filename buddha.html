Under the Tree

A seated figure with five apertures and a stone in his mouth, and then the floor he is sitting on, which has two names. Thirty-two arrangements, seven readings, and one word that changes what a substance can do without changing the substance. no script †

He is not corrupt. Nobody bought a lie — a lie can be checked, and a checkable thing is a liability. He is on a retainer, which does not buy a sentence, it buys a full mouth, and it does not lapse when the mouth closes. There is no false statement anywhere in the instrument. That is why it survives an audit, and it is the reason to draw it rather than describe it: an open mouth reads as speech to everyone walking past, and it is the exact configuration in which no speech is possible.

Six gauges and one of them is arithmetic the others are not. Sight, hearing, ground, assent and held are each one bit — a single :has() and a counter-increment. Speech needs two bits agreeing, mouth open and stone out, and CSS has no AND over counters — so it is a compound selector, :has(#mouth:checked):not(:has(#stone:checked)), which is a lookup table standing in for arithmetic. The seven readings are the same thing at greater length. See workbook.html, chapter seven.

Sixteen of the thirty-two arrangements produce a word, which is half. Speech is not rare; it is the second-commonest state a mouth has. What is rare is a mouth that is open and empty at the same moment somebody is offering to fill it.

The floor what he eats — and it is the same matter under both names

He is under a tree, so he eats what everything under a tree eats. The forest floor runs on what falls and what is passed: the beetle rolls it and keeps the hour by it, the fungus threads it into the network that feeds the tree, the seed rides out inside it, and the tree is built from it. That is not a poetic reading of the floor. It is the floor’s balance sheet, and it is ecosystem.html and mycelium.html stated in one line.

The matter counter reads 1 on both sides. The other two do not. That is the whole finding and it is checkable rather than argued: one substance, one word of difference, and four capabilities become four line items. Nothing was destroyed. The capabilities are still physically there under the second name — the beetle would still eat it — they are just unreachable, because it has been hauled somewhere the beetle is not.

This is theory.md’s argument arriving from underneath. A syndicate does not need to destroy a commons. Destroying things is expensive, visible and eventually illegal. It only needs the commons renamed, and then the removal is somebody’s contract, renewable, with a schedule of fees. The de-fungibilisation is done in the noun. Nobody had to touch the floor.

SPQR

Senatus Populusque Romanus — the Senate and the People, two names, carried on the same standard for a thousand years by an arrangement in which one of them voted and both of them were told the standard meant them. The conjunction is doing every bit of the work. This page is about a noun; that one was about an and.
