The Interior

Crust to core, carried as the ranges the measurements actually support — and the hurricane bowl question answered with a Rossby number, which turns out to have a specific and checkable answer. no script †

Nobody has ever put a seismometer on Venus. Every figure below the surface is a model output constrained by gravity, mass and moment of inertia, so the crust is 20–50 km depending on whose inversion you take and the core is 2,800–3,500 km depending on the model. Both are printed as ranges. A single number here would be a precision nobody has.

The strangest fact on this page is an absence. Venus is the same size as Earth, made of roughly the same things, and has no magnetic field at all. A dynamo needs a convecting core, and convection needs a temperature difference across it. Venus appears not to have one. It has a core and does not use it — and that is also why the atmosphere has been stripped of hydrogen over billions of years with nothing deflecting the solar wind.

3 · The bowl a dust bowl at any size · a hurricane at 2,400 km

Dig a basin and the floor gets hot on its own — 9.5 K a kilometre, so five kilometres of digging buys 48 K of contrast, and 48 K drives convection without anything being burned. That part works. What digging does not buy is rotation.

So the bowl works and the hurricane does not fit in it. Below about 2,400 km the Rossby number is greater than one and you get a thermally direct cell — hot floor, rising column, return flow down the walls — which is a dust bowl in the literal sense, and a good one, because 65 kg/m³ of air at 1 m/s pushes like 7.4 m/s at home. It will sort and move material for as long as the floor stays hotter than the rim, which is for as long as the hole exists.

And the check on the arithmetic is already sitting on the planet: Venus’s only rotating storms are the two polar vortices, and they are about 2,000 km across — the scale this calculation says is the smallest thing that can rotate. Nobody arranged that. It falls out of a 243-day spin, and it is the reason to believe the number rather than the reason to doubt it.

Which is the same shape as the exchange rate: the grand version is barred by a constant, the small version is available now, and the small version was the one that was wanted. You cannot throw an ocean and you can condense a river. You cannot dig a hurricane and you can dig a dust bowl — and a dust bowl over a river is a floodplain, which is the thing that grows a forest.
