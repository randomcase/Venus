Deliberately not pegged. It would be one line to write "1 SLIME = 4 HEZE" and it would be inventing a fact. The two units do different jobs in these files and never have to meet; a rate between them would be a number with nothing behind it, which is the exact thing every other board here refuses to print.

What this is not

Not a token, not a wallet in the crypto sense, and not anything you can hold. There is no chain, no contract, no key, no balance stored anywhere — the figures above are CSS counters summed from the boxes you ticked, and they are gone when the tab closes. SLIME and HEZE are denominations inside this project, ₿1 is a stated franchise price in the manifest, and none of the three implies an exchange rate to any currency. Nothing here is an offer, a solicitation, or financial advice.
