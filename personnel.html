State detachments
R1 — ready. Every billet on the books is filled. All watches can be closed up at once and held there; the board reports no gap and the corporal has nothing outstanding at the rear guard.
R2 — ready, reduced. She sails, fights and keeps station, but not all three at once for long. Watches rotate two in three; anything requiring the whole ship's company closed up comes at the cost of rest. The gap is the number above, and it is on the sergeant.
R3 — steaming party only. Enough hands to move her and keep her safe alongside. Not enough to fight her, and not enough to do both. The commandant is required to say so in plain terms rather than report a percentage.

Everything of import, in one return: what she eats, drinks, burns, holds and breaks. Fuel and ammunition are held figures rather than daily burn — the commandant is signing for what is aboard, not for what was used. The last two lines are the run itself, sized to the cycle you set: three days astern, or six.
