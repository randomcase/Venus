The consolethe counsel aboard; it answers from what the decks saved in this browser, through the ledger server on port 7332

The editorany template on the ship, loaded, changed, kept in this browser, and handed back as a file

Customizations live in this browser under custom.v1, keyed by path. The quarter reads its own back: change a room or a piece here and it changes on the plan. The rulebooks in templates-rules/ are read by their decks the same way: keep a changed one and that deck plays by it on its next load. The themes in templates-theme/ are the ship's look, and they are the one exception: the look is stamped CSS so that pages with no script still have none, which means a changed theme needs node toonami-all.mjs to show. The download is the file to write into the yard, and the generators do the rest.

The plan is on the bridge. Shelves from the forms, chairs and benches from the fabrics, looms from the loom, benches from the instruments, pigeonholes from the shelf. 28094 templates on disk at build. ← the arcade
