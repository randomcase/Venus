A circulatory vote on Motion 12. Six seats, six practices, three instruments, one circuit. You do not get to tell anyone how to vote — you get to decide how the vote is held. no script

Motion 12 before the chamber, every day

That the yard ask every hand, every day, how they are — and stand still for the answer.

Carried at 7. The ayes must reach seven of the weight cast and must outweigh the nays. Below seven the motion is short even if nobody opposed it; level with the nays it is hung and comes round again. The chamber does not adjourn and takes no days off, which is most of what the motion is about — a question asked only when someone looks unwell is a different question.

The register · hats split

On this circuit seats 2 and 5 disagree, and they are the same hand. One person has cast one aye and one nay, at a combined weight of five, and the chamber has counted both because the chamber counts seats. It is not a contradiction and nobody has cheated: the Purser and the Underwriter genuinely answer to different things, and this is one of the two circuits out of twelve where the two things point opposite ways. It is only visible because the register is open.

The ceremony three acts · of 3

None of these three moves a single vote, and the tally above is correct whether you tick them or not. What they decide is whether the correct tally is a decision. That distinction is the whole difference between counting people and governing with them, and here it is enforced by one rule sitting at the bottom of the stylesheet.

The verdict twelve circuits, twelve rules

Carried

The ayes reach the mark and outweigh the nays. From tomorrow the yard asks every hand how they are and stands still for the answer — including on the days when nobody looks like they need asking, which is the reason for putting it on a circuit instead of on a complaint.

Short

The ayes lead and do not reach seven. Nobody in this room opposes the motion on its merits; it is failing on the shape of the sitting. Move one instrument. The votes you need are already in the chamber and have been the whole time.

Hung

Level weight, and no casting vote — the Chair spent hers on the way in. Nothing is decided and nothing is lost. The ring turns and the same motion comes round again tomorrow, which is what a circulation is for and why hanging a circulatory vote is not the same as killing it.

Lost

Read as ceremony, the motion loses every time. The Yardmaster will not be processed at, the Underwriter reads ritual as a cost with no term attached, and the Chair has stood down to preside over it. A motion about asking people a plain question does not survive being asked ceremonially.

Unsealed · rehearsal only

The chamber has counted and the count is correct. It is not a decision: the three acts are not done. Finish the ceremony above and the verdict this circuit produces will be released.

What a syndicate does instead the thing this page is not

A syndicate does not hold votes. It matriculates funds upward and promotes from within, and those two motions are the same motion seen from either end. Because it is cyclical it reproduces — not a copy of itself, but a body identitarianly aligned with itself, mimicking the structure closely enough to be run the same way by the same people. Less amoeba than germ: cohesive as one organism, factional in its parts, and the factions are hats rather than people. Nothing in it is love, or sex, or survival. It is a cycle that learned to make another of itself, and the reason it looks transparent once you have seen it is that it was never hiding — it simply has no step at which anyone is asked.

That step is this whole page. Twelve circuits, six practices, a tally that can be checked against the seats that produced it, and a ceremony that decides whether a correct count is a decision. The register at seats 2 and 5 is the one place where the other structure shows through, and the page names it rather than netting it off. See also the syndicate.

Two of the twelve circuits are worth finding before you find a winning one: the circuit where the chamber hangs dead level, and the one where the motion passes eleven to nothing without a single person having been persuaded of anything.
