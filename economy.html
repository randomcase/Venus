The Twelve

The Olympian pantheon as twelve sectors of an economy, and the order they have to be started in — which is not the order anybody expects, and is the whole content of the page.

Why gods the domains are already attested

Demeter is grain. Hephaestus is the forge. Hermes is roads, exchange and messages. Hera is oath and the household as an institution, which is contract law. Apollo is measure, medicine and prophecy, which is records and standards. Not one of these is stretched. They are the list a society made of the things it could not do without, written down long before anybody had the word “sector”.

Tick them and watch. Every capability below is a conjunction — it names the sectors it actually needs and vanishes when any of them goes dark. Most of the twelve are useless until two or three specific others exist, and finding out which is the exercise.

The twelve colour-coded by where they can start

What works counted from the panels, not declared

WantingAphrodite

Somebody wants something. It requires nothing else and nothing else works without it, which is why it is drawn as a root and why leaving it out of a restart plan is the most common way of producing a plan that describes a prison rather than an economy.

The commonsArtemis

There is something not yet developed. She is the only sector that shrinks as the others grow — every other capability on this page is spending her — and she is the only one whose health you cannot read off her own activity, because her activity is that nothing is happening.

SurviveHestia · Demeter · Poseidon

Shelter, food, water. This is the floor and it is three sectors, not one, and the third is the one people leave out because on Earth it arrives by itself.

MakeHephaestus · Demeter

Tools exist, and tools that make tools. It requires the surplus first: the forge is paid for in meals somebody else grew, and that transfer is the oldest one there is.

TeachAthena · Demeter

A technique outlives the person holding it. Also bought with food: a day spent teaching is a day not spent growing, and every apprenticeship in history has been funded that way whether or not anybody said so.

DefendAres · Demeter

There is something worth protecting and somebody protecting it. Note the direction: the surplus comes first and the force is a response to it. A restart that budgets security before it has anything to secure has described a garrison.

TradeHermes · Hephaestus · Demeter

Two producers with different surpluses and a road between them. One producer is not a market. This is the first capability on the page that needs three sectors, and it is where an economy stops being a household.

AccountApollo · Hermes

Writing was invented for this. Cuneiform does not begin with poetry, law or scripture. It begins as commodity tallies — so many measures of barley, so many head of livestock — pressed into clay because somebody had to hold a quantity across time and could not.

Which fixes the order and settles an argument that is otherwise a matter of taste: the ledger is downstream of the trade. Records are a consequence of exchange rather than a precondition for it, Apollo comes after Hermes, and the first thing anybody ever wrote down was a receipt.

Five thousand years later this repository is doing the same thing, and so is every ship in the fleet: counting something in a form that can be checked by somebody who was not there. The countersigned log and the clay tablet are one invention.

BindHera · Apollo

An agreement survives the moment of making it. It needs the record: an oath nobody can produce is a memory, and two memories that disagree is the condition contract law exists to end.

AdjudicateZeus · Hera · Apollo

A dispute can be settled by something other than who is stronger. It needs a rule and it needs a record, and the authority is the least of the three — which is visible here because Zeus is the only sector that contributes nothing until two others are already running.

Rule by memoryZeus, no Apollo

Authority with no record. Every judgment is correct on the day it is made and unavailable afterwards, so the same dispute is settled differently in the same year by the same person, honestly. This is not corruption. It is what adjudication is in the absence of writing, and it is the state cuneiform was invented to leave.

Force with nothing to protectAres, no Demeter

Armed and hungry. Security funded before there is a surplus does not produce safety, it produces the only group with the means to take what little there is. The ordering is not a nicety.

A market with one sellerHermes, no forge

Roads, messages and exchange, and only one kind of thing to exchange. That is not trade; it is distribution, and it will be described as a market for as long as somebody benefits from the description.

A forge nobody can feedHephaestus, no Demeter

A smith works a day and does not grow food that day. Without the surplus the forge is a person choosing between their craft and their supper, and the craft loses every time, which is why the first thing a civilisation does with grain is stop everybody having to grow it.

Nothing runningall twelve dark

Not a ruin — a beginning. Four of the twelve are roots and can be started from here with nothing upstream of them: wanting, water, shelter and the commons. Everything else on the page is waiting on one of those four.

Sumer where this actually happened

The first cities, the first surplus, the first bureaucracy and the first writing, in that order, in the same place. And the order is the point: writing arrives fourth, after there is something to count, somebody to count it for, and a distance across which the count has to survive.

Tokens in clay envelopes came before symbols. Then the envelope was marked on the outside so it did not have to be broken to be read, and then somebody noticed the tokens inside were now redundant. That is the whole invention: a mark that stands for a quantity, made because a container had to be readable without being opened. Literature is a much later use of the same tool and would not have paid for its development.

What follows for a restart. Do not plan to install records early. Plan to install trade, and the records will be demanded by the people doing it, in the form they actually need, which will not be the form anybody designed in advance. Every accounting system that was specified before the exchange it accounts for has had to be replaced by one the traders wrote themselves.
