Consistency

does the yard still agree with itself

Every layer here is checked by its own generator. Nothing was checking whether the layers agree with each other — and across 219 pages, 30 data layers and 60 generators, that is now the likelier failure. A knowledge-base entry that quotes a figure has copied it: add forty houses and the prose is quietly false, the entry keeps its settled badge, and nothing objects. Prose does not recompute. This does.

No faults. Every watched figure quoted in prose matches the layer that owns it, every page anything cites exists, and the book is priced in a coin that is actually defined. A gap is different and is not a bug: something incomplete that the yard already knows about and reports rather than hides. The distinction is the whole value of this page — a checker that calls everything an error gets switched off, so this one exits clean and never halts a build.
